[Showdeck](https://showdeck.io/) describes itself as a workplace collaboration tool for the freelance creative industry: one place for the people who put on live performances (performers, directors, designers, technicians, venue and production staff) to create, organise, and keep the information a production runs on, instead of scattering it across a dozen tools.

I've been working on it as a contractor since May 2026, fullstack rather than one slice of it.

That's deliberately all that's here. The engagement is covered by confidentiality, which includes what I've learned about the codebase, the architecture, and how the team operates. Work that stays confidential is worth more to the next client than a case study is.

How I work day to day is my own to describe, and [the about page](/about) covers it. Happy to talk it through directly: [john@johnmoorman.com](mailto:john@johnmoorman.com).
